# My Favorite Anime: Dr. Stone

**Dr. Stone** is a science adventure anime about rebuilding civilization after humanity is mysteriously turned to stone.

## Why I Like It

- It makes science and inventions exciting.
- Senku and his friends solve problems through curiosity and teamwork.
- The story mixes adventure, humor, and discovery.

### Favorite Character

**Senku Ishigami** stands out for his determination and love of science.

> The series shows how knowledge and cooperation can help people overcome big challenges.
